Quick Start: The 3 Pillars of Improvement

If you only remember three things from this guide, remember these:

  • Solve Tactics Daily - 10-20 puzzles minimum. Pattern recognition is the fastest path to rating gains.
  • Play Longer Time Controls - 15+10 minimum. Blitz is fun but prevents serious improvement.
  • Analyze Every Game - Spend 10-20 minutes reviewing your games, focusing on 2-3 key mistakes.
